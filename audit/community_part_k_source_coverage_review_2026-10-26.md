# CHARLS 2011 Community Questionnaire Part K source coverage review

## Scope and evidence

Reviewed all seven substantive items in Part K (Interviewer Observations) of the official 2011 Community Questionnaire against the official Codebook and metadata-only raw field inventory. No participant answers or Codebook frequency summaries were used. The questionnaire instructs interviewers to observe the community, but individual item wording must still determine the measurement level.

## Dispositions

| Source fields | Disposition | Meaning and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| JK001 | E candidate, already mapped | Whether a road passes through the village/community; 2011 community context. |
| JK002 | E candidate, already mapped | Interviewer 1–7 judgment from poor to rich; subjective area context, not household income or an objective economic statistic. |
| JK003 | E candidate, already mapped | Interviewer 1–7 judgment from very dirty to very tidy; subjective road condition, not environmental sampling. |
| JK004 | E candidate, already mapped | Interviewer 1–7 judgment from very disorganized to very organized; subjective built-form proxy, not a standardized urban-form measure. |
| JK005 | E candidate, already mapped | Interviewer 1–7 judgment from very crowded to very sparse; not measured population density. |
| JK006 | E candidate, already mapped | Interviewer 1–7 judgment from no disability access to very convenient; not an accessibility audit. |
| JK007 | Excluded from E/P candidate concepts; retain as source/informant metadata if available | The item explicitly rates the Mandarin fluency of the Part K “respondent.” It is a characteristic of the community-questionnaire informant/interviewee, not a community environmental attribute and not necessarily a CHARLS cohort member's language proficiency. The community survey's 454-row inventory also differs from the respondent-level roster; no person-level link or identity equivalence is established. Do not use it as an individual covariate without a verified, authorized linkage and target-person definition. |

The six mapped items remain separate, single-wave, community-level candidates. Their shared section header does not override JK007's informant-specific wording. No composite score, population density, objective pollution measure, or participant-level language variable is inferred.

## Verification limits

The raw inventory confirms that fields JK001–JK007 exist in the local metadata inventory (module dimensions only). It does not prove participant-level availability, community linkage, release coding for JK007, valid analytic denominators, or survey-design suitability. Those remain separate gates. Frequencies and respondent values are not included in the public audit.
