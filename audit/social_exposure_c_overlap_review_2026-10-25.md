Reinspection confirmed that the Codebook's PDF text extraction replaces the underscore before the suffix with a space (`da057 8`), and the exact normalized heading is present. The source-backed link is now included. The wave-specific label and route discrepancies remain explicit; no later-wave response or route was borrowed for 2011.
