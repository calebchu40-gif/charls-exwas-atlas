# Wave 1 pilot cross-variant stability (methods only)

**Status:** exploratory, PI-delegated, not independently reviewed, and not approved for publication. Scores, ranking, coefficients, uncertainty estimates, and all result-derived counts remain in the protected server workspace.

## Scoring method

The private analysis compares each registered hypothesis across the prespecified sensitivity variants. The fasting-adjustment variant applies only to glucose and triglycerides; other hypotheses have a different set of applicable variants. Any stability score is descriptive of this one dataset and these related model specifications. It is not an independent replication, multiplicity correction, or basis for selecting hypotheses.

The ranking output is `private/results/wave1_pilot105_stability_v1.csv`. It is not copied to the public repository or site. Do not use p-values or cross-variant stability to choose pairings for confirmatory analysis.

## Interpretation boundary

All pilot associations remain exploratory until the estimand, outcome rules, design approximation, model, multiplicity family, reproducibility, and disclosure controls have been independently reviewed. A stable direction across related specifications does not establish causality or external validity.
