# Family economic transfer candidate mapping review (2026-10-25)

This review adds family economic support transfers as an exposure-candidate domain. It maps fields from the official CHARLS questionnaires and wave-specific Codebooks into six directional/relationship groups. It does not create totals, scores, cross-wave harmonized values, or analysis-ready variables.

## Mapped concepts

| Concept ID | Direction and roster | Field families | Waves |
|---|---|---|---|
| `economic_support_received_parents` | Respondent/spouse receives from non-co-resident parent roster | `CE002_*` | 2011, 2013, 2015, 2018 |
| `economic_support_received_parents_in_law` | Respondent/spouse receives from non-co-resident parents-in-law | `CE005_*` | 2011, 2013 |
| `economic_support_received_children` | Respondent/spouse receives from non-co-resident children | `CE009_*` | 2011, 2013, 2015, 2018 |
| `economic_support_provided_parents` | Respondent/spouse provides to non-co-resident parent roster | `CE022_*` | 2011, 2013, 2015, 2018 |
| `economic_support_provided_parents_in_law` | Respondent/spouse provides to non-co-resident parents-in-law | `CE025_*` | 2011, 2013 |
| `economic_support_provided_children` | Respondent/spouse provides to non-co-resident children | `CE029_*` | 2011, 2013, 2015, 2018 |

Mappings retain the exact raw field and wave, including person-indexed child/parent slots, regularity interval fields, and unfolding-bracket bounds where those are in the raw catalog. These are not interchangeable. A bracket lower/upper bound is not an exact transfer amount, and no aggregation across family-member slots is supplied.

## Source interpretation and limitations

- Official 2011 and 2013 questionnaires separately distinguish parents and parents-in-law in the CE002/CE005 and CE022/CE025 sections. The 2015/2018 catalogs do not provide the same distinct CE005/CE025 field families; this review does not infer that later parent-roster fields identify in-laws.
- 2015/2018 use roster-indexed fields. Eligibility depends on the household/family roster and non-co-residence rules. The atlas preserves candidate source coverage, not the eligible denominator.
- Official questionnaire wording describes past-year transfers and separates money/in-kind and regular/non-regular support. Exact field numbering and labels vary by wave.
- The 2011 Baseline Questionnaire and Codebook do not consistently align on CE sub-item numbering/labels. 2011 fields remain candidate mappings with an explicit release-level reconciliation warning. No values or Codebook frequencies are copied into the atlas.
- The E role means a candidate social/family context measure. Whether a field is an exposure, mediator, outcome, or covariate depends on a future estimand; this mapping does not select an adjustment set or approve statistical analysis.
- No respondent-level data, counts, or estimates were used or added.

## Provenance and validation

The machine-readable source crosswalk is [`social_exposure_crosswalk_v1.csv`](social_exposure_crosswalk_v1.csv), generated by [`build_social_exposure_crosswalk.py`](../scripts/build_social_exposure_crosswalk.py). The records point to the official extracted questionnaires and Codebooks under `sources_official/`. Regression checks in [`test_social_economic_support_crosswalk.py`](../tests/test_social_economic_support_crosswalk.py) verify exact raw-catalog membership, six distinct relationship/direction concepts, no unsupported later-wave in-law equivalence, and retained 2011 and bracket caveats.
