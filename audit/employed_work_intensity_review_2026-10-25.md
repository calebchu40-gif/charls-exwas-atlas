# Employed-work days and hours review — 2026-10-25

Reviewed 10 official field-wave entries: average days worked per week (`FE002`, 2011–2018; `FC026`, 2020) and average hours worked per working day (`FE003`, 2011–2018; `FC027`, 2020). Both refer to past-year wage-employment work. Hours exclude meal breaks and include paid/unpaid overtime where specified. The units are separate continuous numeric measures; no ordinal category labels or composite workload score are inferred.

Wave-specific rules remain visible. In 2018, irregular weekly workdays may be derived from annual workdays divided by working months and four. In 2020, weeks with paid vacations and fully paid sick leave are excluded from the days-per-week average; interviewers are instructed to record 1 when work occurs fewer than four days per month and the average is below one day per week. These rules, differing wage-work referents and eligibility routes mean the same unit does not establish measurement equivalence. These are work-intensity candidates, not direct environmental dose measures. Confirm release storage, special values, denominator and person linkage before analysis.
