# Toilet flushability review

Reviewed the toilet-flushability items in the official 2011, 2013, 2015, 2018 and 2020 questionnaires and wave Codebooks. The fields are `I015` in 2011–2018 and `I014` in 2020; the listed response categories are 1=Yes and 2=No.

The 2015 and 2018 questionnaires route a seated-toilet response past the flushability item. Keep that routed eligibility distinct from No and verify how the authorized release stores structural skips. The 2011, 2013 and 2020 question wording reviewed here does not show that same skip. Do not pool across waves until response storage, eligible denominators, household-person linkage and missingness are checked.

For 2013 and 2015, the local extracted questionnaire text omitted the relevant housing pages. The original PDFs were checked directly: 2013 printed page 259 (PDF page 265), and 2015 printed pages 224–225 (PDF pages 230–231). The brief English extraction is `sources_official/CHARLS_2013_2015_housing_questionnaire_pages_extracted.txt`; it records the question, response options, and the 2015 skip. The complete official PDFs remain the primary sources. No participant records, values, Codebook frequencies or analytic results were used.
