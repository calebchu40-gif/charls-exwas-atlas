# Community industrial-pollution candidate source review (2011)

Updated 2026-10-24. This review uses the official 2011 Community Questionnaire and Codebook only; no respondent records, response counts or Codebook descriptive statistics are included.

The existing concept group contains three community-informant fields with different referents and data types:

| Field | Source meaning | Scale / unit | Interpretation boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| `JB018` | Whether industrial pollution is present in the respondent's village/community | Four ordered questionnaire anchors: very severe, some, almost none, completely absent | Subjective community report. Higher response code means less reported pollution. Not an instrument-measured concentration. |
| `JB019` | Whether nearby villages or townships have industrial pollution | Same four ordered anchors | CAPI route is village-only. Its referent is nearby settlements, not the respondent's own residence. Higher response code means less reported pollution. |
| `JB027` | Distance from the village to the closest industrial pollution site | Kilometres; the questionnaire explicitly says to answer 0 if the source is located in the village | CAPI route is village-only. Zero is a meaningful in-village location rule, not automatically missing or “no pollution”. The distance is village-to-site, not respondent-home-to-site. |

These fields remain separate source components under one searchable community pollution concept; no composite pollution score is created. The ratings and distance are contextual proxies with distinct routes and interpretation. None is a personal exposure concentration, biomarker, or validated dose. Before analysis, verify respondent eligibility, release-specific special values, the community-to-person linkage and clustering/design implications. No association or sample-size claim follows from the catalogue mapping.
