# Social-care caregiver metadata review (2011 and 2013)

Fourteen candidate fields were matched to the 2011/2013 official questionnaires and Codebooks: 13 routed helper-option fields and one helper-days count. The 2011 questionnaire identifies volunteer/facility workers as helper options (including a note that facility employees apply to nursing-home contexts); the 2013 instrument identifies volunteer/facility assistance in current helper questions and future-helper questions. The Codebook locates each wave-field and selected option code, including `No Observations` for 2011 `db022s38`; those summaries are not participant-level evidence about unselected storage.

The site therefore leaves response categories blank and displays that the affirmative helper-option code is known while unselected coding and eligible denominator remain unresolved. A missing/blank slot must not be classified as “No.” The 2011 `db023_38_` item is a continuous days measure for help received, with the conditional helper route retained; the Codebook's summaries are not thresholds. No frequencies were copied.

The 14-row review is reproducible with `scripts/apply_social_caregiver_metadata_review.py`. The catalog builder imports these source-specific review states. This does not establish analysis eligibility or caregiver exposure harmonization.
