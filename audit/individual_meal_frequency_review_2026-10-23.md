# Individual usual meal frequency (2011 and 2013)

The 2011 and 2013 DA058 questionnaires ask how many meals the respondent normally eats each day and provide the same six ordered choices: more than four, four, three, two, one, or fewer than one meal per day. The labels are recorded as ordered self-report categories; no category frequencies from the Codebooks are reproduced.

This is an individual behavior item, not a measure of food quality, calories, nutrients, household food expenditure, or laboratory exposure. The catalog contains the item in 2011 and 2013 only; this does not establish later-wave coverage or permit imputation across waves. Special values, item eligibility and missingness still require release-specific QA before analysis. The reproducible application is `scripts/apply_individual_meal_frequency_review.py` and is run in both catalog pipelines.
