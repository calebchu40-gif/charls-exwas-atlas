# Core anthropometric source review (2011–2015)

## Scope and sources

Reviewed CHARLS 2011 physical-examination questionnaire and baseline user guide, and the 2013/2015 Biomarker Questionnaires and wave Codebooks. This is a source-metadata review only. No respondent measurements, Codebook frequency tables, or derived BMI/health classifications are used or published.

## Measures

Standing height is recorded in cm; upper-arm length and knee height are also in cm, with the measured arm and leg retained as separate context fields. Body weight is recorded in kg and has a questionnaire-defined eligibility route at 150 kg; eligibility, footwear, compliance and measurement quality remain separate fields. Waist circumference is recorded in cm, with wave-specific clothing and completion context retained. The source documents 993/999 noncompletion or status codes for these measurements; do not treat them as numeric values. Routed or unasked measurements are not zero.

## Field identity and analytical limits

The 2011 physical-examination instrument uses LI011, LI015, LI019, LI841 and LI907 for these measurements, while release Codebook candidates use QI/QJ/QK/QL/QM identifiers. This review does not assert exact one-to-one release-field identity. The 2013/2015 questionnaires and Codebooks document the corresponding QI002/QJ002/QK002/QL002/QM002 fields, but this does not establish availability in an authorized participant-level release.

Units and instrument wording do not establish analytic validity. Before modeling, verify release-field identity, valid/missing-code implementation, body-weight eligibility, measurement context and cross-wave comparability. BMI is not derived by this catalog review.
