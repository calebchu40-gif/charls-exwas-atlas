# 2020 dwelling move-in time versus construction year

The 2020 Housing Characteristics questionnaire defines `i002` as the year the Household Financial Respondent moved into the current housing. If the respondent cannot provide an exact year, `i003` records an elapsed-time interval (0–5, 5–10, 10–20, 20–30, 30–40, or more than 40 years). The corresponding Codebook headings are “Time House Built” and “Time Interval House Built,” but the questionnaire's explicit item wording controls interpretation here: these items measure respondent move-in timing, not the building's construction date.

The prior food/environment crosswalk incorrectly placed `i002`/`i003` under “Year dwelling was built.” This review moves both fields to a separate `residence_move_in_time` concept and retains exact year and interval as alternative response fields. The fields must not be combined without a validated format/route rule or reinterpreted as dwelling age. The 2011–2018 `i005` / `i005_1` items continue to represent when the house was built; they remain distinct from 2020's move-in measure. The 2020 exact-year question gives a hard-coded year range and interviewer instruction to enter -1 if unknown; this does not by itself establish every special-value or eligible-sample rule in the released analytic file.

Sources checked: `sources_official/CHARLS_2020_Questionnaire_English_extracted.txt` (I002–I003) and `sources_official/CHARLS_2020_Codebook_extracted.txt` (`i002`, `i003`). No participant-level records or Codebook frequencies were used. The updated crosswalk is rebuilt by `scripts/build_food_environment_crosswalk.py` and then propagated through the standard metadata pipeline.
