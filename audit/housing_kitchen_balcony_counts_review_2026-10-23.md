# Household kitchen and balcony counts

Eight fields in 2011, 2013, 2015 and 2018 record the number of kitchens and balconies in the sampled household residence. These are numeric household built-environment counts, not discrete categories or individual exposure doses. The questionnaires place a soft check above 20; this is an interviewer prompt, not a validated analytic cutoff. Codebook extrema do not define valid ranges.

The repeated item names and wording support retaining the source fields by wave, but do not by themselves prove equivalence of the sampled residence or household roster across waves. Check authorized-release special values, residence linkage and missingness before modeling. No participant values or Codebook frequencies were used. Metadata propagation is reproducible through `scripts/apply_housing_kitchen_balcony_review.py` in both catalog pipelines.
