# 2014 WORKHISTORY F101a agricultural income review

## Sources and scope

Reviewed F101a/F101a_a definitions and routing in the public 2014 CHARLS Life History Questionnaire and Codebook. This is a metadata-only review; no participant responses, income values, frequencies, eligible sample sizes or associations were inspected or produced.

## Distinct concepts and coverage

The 49 Codebook fields are separated into four concepts, retaining job slot and period:

- 13 beginning-period payment-mode fields (`f101a_*`): yearly net income in Yuan versus workpoints;
- 13 beginning-period Yuan amount fields (`f101a_1_*`), conditional on selecting Yuan;
- 11 middle-period payment-mode fields (`f101a_a_*`); and
- 12 middle-period Yuan amount fields (`f101a_a_1_*`), conditional on selecting Yuan.

All are limited to agricultural work episodes (F_3=1). Middle-period questions additionally require an episode lasting at least 20 years; the available slot sets differ from beginning-period coverage. The questionnaire defines net income as total income less agricultural input costs (e.g. seed, fertilizer and pesticide) and includes the market value of farm products consumed at home. This is a retrospective economic measure. It does not indicate use of pesticides or other environmental agents.

## Interpretation and analysis limits

Do not treat the Yuan/workpoint choice as an ordinal scale. A skip after selecting workpoints is not zero Yuan income. Nominal yearly amounts from different historical periods are not inflation-adjusted and should not be pooled without a justified price-year/comparability method. Preserve episode and timing; verify any numeric ranges, special codes and release-specific storage before analysis.

Local rebuild: 135 concepts / 4,595 source-field mappings (E=4,549, P=46); WORKHISTORY 1,754 mapped candidate fields and 233 substantive fields deferred. Counts represent metadata rows, not analysis-ready indicators or sample sizes. Formal association models remain zero.
