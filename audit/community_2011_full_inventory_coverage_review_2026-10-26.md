## Newly mapped Part F fields: JF003 facility geographic scope

Added the 32 `JF003_{level}_{facility}_` source fields as a separate community healthcare-access concept. The fields describe whether a selected, commonly used facility is within or outside the current province, county/city, township/district, and village/street; later levels are conditionally routed. Questionnaire and Codebook category wording is retained per exact field, with frequencies omitted. These categories are relative community context, not participant utilization, an exact address, a distance measure, or geocodable location. Keep facility slots and levels separate; do not collapse them to one ordinal score before authorized-release route/coding checks. JF006 distance-to-community-office remains a separate measure.

## C-only Part H/J/K fields

- Part H: JH001 meeting date/time/attendance and JH002 education/gender/age fields describe the history-interview meeting or its 2–3 knowledgeable informants, not the sampled CHARLS participants. JH031 is conditional free text describing policy terms. All are C-only; informant values and open-text responses are not published.
- Part J: JJ034 is the informant position and is C-only. JJ036, JJ037 slots, and JJ038 channel/percentage slots appear in the Codebook, but matching item wording and route were not found in the official Questionnaire. They remain C-only unresolved fields; no credit construct, unit, or response interpretation is inferred.
- Part K: JK007 is interviewer-observed Mandarin fluency of the community respondent. It is not a participant characteristic or environmental measure and remains C-only.
- `communityID` and `sub_commuid` are linkage/structural keys and are excluded from the E/P matrix and public field catalog.

## Build and safeguards

JF003 mappings are generated by `scripts/build_community_exposure_crosswalk.py`; exact source-field answer labels are extracted by `scripts/build_community_value_labels.py`, whose label parser removes frequency tails. C-only dispositions are applied from `audit/catalog_role_override_review_v1.csv`. The 719-field coverage and JF003 mapping are checked in `tests/test_community_inventory_disposition_coverage.py`. All mappings remain candidates with analytical eligibility pending; presence in this metadata catalog does not establish a valid participant-level model or survey-design solution.
