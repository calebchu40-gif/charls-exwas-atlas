# Education and hukou response-label review (2011–2020)

Fourteen wave-field mappings (2011 n=3; 2013 n=4; 2015 n=2; 2018 n=3; 2020 n=2) were checked against the official questionnaire and Codebook. The reviewed fields describe current hukou type/location and education attainment or reported change. Exact wave-specific categories were transcribed without Codebook frequencies.

Education labels are not forced into one identical menu: the 2011/2013 attainment items list codes 1–10; 2013/2015 `bd001_w2_4` includes `12=No change`; the 2018 and 2020 education menus include code 11 for doctoral degree. Hukou-location menus also differ by wave and are kept as distinct categories.

The 2013 Codebook uses the `zbc001`, `zbc005` and `zbd001` aliases for the corresponding questionnaire fields. The atlas records that naming discrepancy and asks users to verify the alias against their authorized release before analysis. Wave-specific route, sample eligibility, special codes, and cross-wave construct equivalence remain analysis-stage checks. No respondent data or frequencies were used.

Rebuild this review with `scripts/apply_demographic_value_label_review.py`; it is run after the demographic crosswalk in the full pipeline.
