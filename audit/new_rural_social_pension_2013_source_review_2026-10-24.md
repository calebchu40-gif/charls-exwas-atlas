# 2013 New Rural Social Pension participation source review

Reviewed 2013 `FN069_W2` against the official Wave 2 questionnaire and Codebook. The questionnaire asks whether the respondent currently participates in the New Rural Social Pension Insurance program; Codebook response codes are `1=Yes` and `2=No`. A Yes response skips to `FN071_W2` for first-coverage month/year. A No response leads to `FN070_W2`, a multi-select set of non-participation reasons. The source checker reads wording and code labels only; Codebook frequencies are not copied or treated as project sample counts.

The 2013 item is linked to the existing candidate concept `new_rural_social_pension_participation`, which already includes 2011 `FN071`. Both items concern participation in the named scheme, but the catalogue does not claim cross-wave measurement equivalence. Preserve the separate question wording, route and eligible denominator by wave. Do not conflate this item with pension receipt, contribution payments, benefit amount, a preloaded plan roster, or other pension programs. The reason slots after No are conditional metadata and must not be read as independent exposures or recoded from missingness.

The review updates `pension_exposure_variable_mapping_v1.csv` and adds a wave-specific row to `pension_participation_crosswalk_v1.csv`, so the field catalogue and E/P concept-source index can retain both the variable label and exact route caveat. Authorized-release field identity and special/missing-code handling still require release-specific QA. No respondent-level data or Codebook frequency statistics were used.
