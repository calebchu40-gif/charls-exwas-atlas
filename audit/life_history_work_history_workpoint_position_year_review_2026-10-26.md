# 2014 WORKHISTORY F101b/F110_a1 review

## Sources and scope

Reviewed the public 2014 CHARLS Life History Questionnaire and Codebook for the 14 F101b/F101b_a fields and seven F110_a1 fields. This review concerns metadata only; no participant responses, frequencies, sample sizes or association models were generated.

## Agricultural workpoint components

F101b records daily workpoints and the reported Yuan value of one workpoint at the beginning of agricultural work; F101b_a repeats these measures during the middle period. The questions route only to agricultural work (F_3=1); the middle-period fields are within the ≥20-year job-history section. The 14 mappings preserve the two separate components, phase and job slot. They are not converted to realized cash income and are not evidence of pesticide exposure or environmental dose.

## Middle-period employment position transition year

F110_a1 records the year when the respondent began holding the middle-period position, asked only if F110_a differs from F110. The seven fields are mapped separately from rank categories; missing transition year is not interpreted as no rank change. Preserve the F_3=2/3 route, episode and conditional denominator.

## Local catalogue impact

Local rebuild: 147 concepts / 4,743 source mappings (E=4,697, P=46); WORKHISTORY 1,902 mapped candidate fields and 85 substantive fields deferred. Formal association models remain zero.
