# 2014 WORKHISTORY F123a/F123b agricultural current/last-year income review

## Sources and scope

Reviewed official 2014 Life History Questionnaire and Codebook entries for F123a/F123b. No participant-level responses, income distributions, frequencies, sample sizes or association results were examined or generated.

## Routing and mapping

The questionnaire asks F123a1–F123c only for agricultural jobs (F_3=1) lasting at least five years. F123a asks current/the-last-year personal net yearly agricultural income; the definition deducts input costs and includes market value of farm products consumed at home. The respondent selects Yuan or workpoints. The 32 deferred fields are now split into three concepts: 13 income-mode fields, 13 conditional Yuan amount fields and six F123b workpoint components (three job slots by two components: daily points and stated Yuan value per point). The existing F123c work-intensity concept remains separate.

## Interpretation limits

Preserve the original “now/the last year” reference wording and job slot. Do not assume a uniform calendar reference or convert workpoint components into cash income without validating coding and assumptions. Yuan values remain nominal, not inflation-harmonized. Agricultural income does not establish pesticide use, chemical contact or environmental dose.

## Local catalogue impact

Local rebuild: 141 concepts / 4,673 mappings (E=4,627, P=46); WORKHISTORY 1,832 mapped candidate fields and 155 substantive fields deferred. These remain metadata counts only; formal association models remain zero.
