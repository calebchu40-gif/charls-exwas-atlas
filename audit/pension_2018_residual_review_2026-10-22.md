# 2018 pension residual field review (2026-10-22)

This review uses the official 2018 Wave 4 questionnaire and Codebook only. No respondent microdata were opened or analyzed.

## Decisions

- Coarse location items are E candidates. This includes receipt location (`fn070_w4`, `fn080_w4`, `fn042_w4`, `fn096_w4`, `fn078_w4`), life-insurance claim receipt location (`fn056_w4`), and plan participation/enrollment location (`fn074_w4`, `fn079_w4`, `fn044_w2`, `fn034_w4`, `fn059_w2`). The common broad response categories are current residence, another township/village/neighborhood within the current-residence county/city/district, another place, and don't know. Preserve the named pension/insurance route and distinguish receipt from participation.
- `fn006_w4_1_`–`fn006_w4_4_` and `fn007_w4_1_`–`fn007_w4_4_` are also E candidates. The Codebook gives each a coarse categorical response distribution, while the questionnaire places them in the repeated formal-plan question loop. The suffix is the plan-loop instance, not a respondent address. Keep each loop instance and its plan denominator distinct; do not collapse them into a single observation.
- `fn058_w4` is a social-pension family screen that combines participation, expected receipt, or current receipt. `fn058_w4_a` records the selected residents/new-rural/urban-residents plan type. They are separate candidate concepts; the screen is not current receipt alone.
- `fn033_w2` is years enrolled in a named plan. The 2018 wording expressly includes years under different employers when enrolled in the same pension program, so retain a 2018-specific concept rather than silently assume the older-wave definition is identical.
- `fn066_w2_1` and `_2` are expected monthly and lump-sum benefits from the selected formal plan. Keep units and routes separate. `fn056_w2_8` is a life-insurance payout already received and remains distinct from pension benefits and expected life-insurance benefits.
- `fn056_w2_6`, `fn056_w2_9`, and `fn079_w2_6` are response-format selectors (payment frequency, expected-benefit form, and monthly/yearly premium form). Reclassify them as C metadata; the unit-specific child fields carry the quantities.
- Bracket bounds for `fn079_w2_6`, `fn079_w2_11`, `fn062_w4_a/b`, `fn056_w2_8/9`, and `fn014_w4_b_3_` are C metadata. They describe interval/unfolding responses and must not be treated as exact monetary values, midpoints, or independent E measures.

All are candidates for later analysis, not evidence of association, analytic sample size, or permission to publish respondent-level data.
