# Written labor contract status review — 2026-10-25

Reviewed current-work labor-contract status in five wave packages: `FD020` (2011–2018) and `FC016` (2020). The Codebooks identify Yes/No responses. The 2011 questionnaire prints the words Yes/No without numeric display codes; those numerical labels come from the Codebook and remain subject to release-level verification.

The referent is not completely uniform: 2011–2015 ask about a written contract from the current workplace or labor-dispatch company; 2018 interviewer instructions specify the dispatch company for dispatched workers and the wage-paying person for workers hired by an individual; 2020 asks whether a labor contract was signed with the current employer. Preserve the wording and routes. A “No” response routes away from contract-duration follow-ups; those structural skips must not be used to infer missing contract status. This is a candidate employment-condition/background variable, not a direct environmental exposure or measure of contract quality. No respondent data or Codebook frequencies were used.

Reproduce checks and synchronization with `python scripts/apply_written_labor_contract_review.py`.
