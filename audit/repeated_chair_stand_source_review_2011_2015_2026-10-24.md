# Repeated chair-stand source review (2011–2015)

## Scope and protocol

Reviewed the 2011 physical-examination questionnaire and baseline user guide, plus 2013/2015 Biomarker Questionnaires and wave Codebooks. The test asks participants to stand up and sit down five times without using their arms. The instrument preamble describes a fastest-pace instruction, while the outcome item describes usual pace; this wording difference is retained as a source discrepancy instead of being harmonized away.

Preserve completion/status, route-specific duration fields, completed stand count (0–5), and trunk/arm use separately. The two duration fields apply to different completion routes and must not be merged or substituted. Chair height/type fields are test setup metadata, not respondent standing height. No summary score or performance association is computed here.

The questionnaires define 993 as tried but unable and 999 as chose not to complete. These are status codes, not times or counts.

## Field identity and limits

2011 instrument items LI003–LI008 correspond conceptually to candidate QH002–QH006 release fields, but the exact one-to-one identity is unresolved. The 2013/2015 questionnaire and Codebook document QH002–QH006. Instrument text does not establish the fields' presence in an authorized participant-level release. Confirm release identity, valid/missing codes, routes, timing implementation and a prespecified score before any analysis.
