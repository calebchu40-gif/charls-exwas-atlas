# Household property-sale allocation: protected storage QA

Reviewed 2026-10-24 using the prepared 2013 and 2015 person analytic tables in the access-controlled local CHARLS workspace. The review reads only the target property status, percentage, and option-selector columns; it does not read identifiers, export respondent-level rows, or publish response values or cell counts. The reproducible runner is `scripts/audit_household_sale_proceeds_storage.py`; its aggregate-only output is written outside the repository under the protected local QA directory.

The prepared tables contain the expected amount, selector, and property-status columns. Nonblank amount strings parsed as numeric and fell within the questionnaire's 0–100 percentage range in the protected aggregate check. Amounts appeared only for records whose corresponding property status was sold. However, the option-selector and percentage fields did not have complete one-to-one presence: some selector responses had no paired amount. This may reflect item nonresponse, routing, release encoding, or an incorrect field linkage; the aggregate check cannot distinguish these explanations.

This is a structural check of locally prepared merged tables, not validation of an authorized original release. The available manifest does not establish the exact source archive identity or checksums, and household-level fields are repeated in person tables. Therefore these checks do not define an analytic denominator, do not justify person-level modeling, and do not establish cross-wave equivalence. Keep all 54 percentage candidates marked `DO NOT ANALYZE`; do not impute missing percentages, treat blank selectors as “not selected,” calculate allocation totals, or use the repeated person-table rows as independent household observations. Before reconsideration, verify the exact source release and hashes, inspect the official storage/skip conventions for selectors and amounts, determine the household-level eligible denominator and duplicate property routing, and validate the mapping against the original household-level files.

The local QA output remains private and is not part of this repository or the Pages site. Tests use synthetic inputs only.
