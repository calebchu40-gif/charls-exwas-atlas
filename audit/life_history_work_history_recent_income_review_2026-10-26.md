# 2014 WORKHISTORY F126 current/last-year income review

## Sources and scope

Reviewed the official 2014 Life History Questionnaire and Codebook for F126. No participant responses or income distributions were examined, and no association models or sample counts were produced.

## Mapping and route

F124–F126 apply to episodes coded F_3=2 or 3. F126 asks for average monthly income “now/the last year of doing this job” after personal income tax and social-insurance contributions. Respondents choose Yuan or workpoints; Yuan amounts are recorded in F126_a1, while the workpoint branch records daily points and stated money value per point. The 46 Codebook fields map to three concepts: income mode (15), conditional monthly Yuan amount (15), and workpoint components (16, covering eight job slots).

## Interpretation limits

The questionnaire wording combines “now” with “the last year of this job”; do not assume a uniform calendar reference date or that every job is still ongoing. Keep job slots, payment mode and the two workpoint components separate. A workpoint skip is not zero income. Do not calculate imputed currency income from the two workpoint fields without validating authorized-release coding and assumptions. Nominal income is not inflation-harmonized. These are socioeconomic context indicators, not environmental-agent exposures.

## Local catalogue impact

Local rebuild: 138 concepts / 4,641 field mappings (E=4,595, P=46); WORKHISTORY 1,800 mapped candidate fields and 187 substantive fields deferred. Metadata counts are not analysis-ready measures, eligible sample sizes or results. Formal association models remain zero.
