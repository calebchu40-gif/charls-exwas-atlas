# CHARLS 2011 Community Questionnaire Part J source coverage review

## Scope

Reviewed the official 2011 Community Questionnaire Part J (Production, Income and Price), printed pp. 34–37 (PDF pp. 37–40), against the committed official Codebook and the local raw field-name inventory. The review covers substantive questionnaire items JJ001–JJ032 and administrative respondent fields JJ033–JJ035. It does not use participant answers or Codebook frequency summaries.

## Dispositions

- All 49 substantive source fields represented in the field inventory for JJ001–JJ032 have a candidate community-context mapping. JJ001 contributes 18 rank- and measure-specific fields; JJ002–JJ004 cover mechanization/rental; JJ005 covers machinery supply origin; JJ006–JJ010 cover fertilizer/seed inputs; JJ011–JJ016 cover village economic/fiscal context; JJ017–JJ025 cover current commodity/utility prices; and JJ026–JJ032 cover housing-market and formal-credit context.
- Added the concept community_agricultural_machinery_supply_origin for JJ005. The three questionnaire categories are retained as nominal labels. The item is conditional on JJ003=Yes; JJ005_1/JJ005_2 are conditional locality text specified in the questionnaire and are not presumed to be standardized or separately released variables.
- JJ033 (respondent name), JJ034 (respondent identity/position), and JJ035 (respondent phone number) are administrative or directly identifying fields and are excluded from E/P candidates and public display.
- The candidate mappings are community-informant context, not individual exposures or household outcomes. Field units, time reference, informant level, and conditional routes remain field-specific; no composite score is created. Modeling eligibility, special-value handling, community linkage, and survey-design suitability remain unresolved.

## Verification

The JJ005 field label is present in the official Codebook and the local inventory; its county/province categories and JJ003 route are stated in the official questionnaire. The public crosswalk stores category labels only, with frequencies omitted. The focused mapping test checks the concept, source field, route caveat, and labels.
