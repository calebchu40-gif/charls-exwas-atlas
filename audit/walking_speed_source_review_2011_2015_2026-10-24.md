# Timed walking source review (2011–2015)

## Scope and evidence

This review covers the CHARLS candidate phenotype fields `qg002` and `qg003` in 2011, 2013, and 2015. It uses only the locally retained official questionnaires, manuals, user guide, and Codebooks under `sources_official/`. No respondent records, distributions, frequencies, or summary statistics were inspected.

The source review confirms a 2.5 m usual-pace timed walk with two recorded trials, measured in seconds. Eligibility and interpretation must retain the questionnaire's age-60-or-older and route/safety conditions. The two trials remain separate source fields; this catalog does not average trials or derive speed.

For the documented response semantics, `993` means attempted but unable to complete and `999` means chose not to do the test. They are status codes, not elapsed times. Structural non-measurement must not be recoded to zero. Confirm special values, exact storage, route eligibility, and the prespecified summary algorithm in the authorized release before any analysis.

The 2011 physical-examination manual uses LI item identifiers while the 2011 Codebook uses QG variable names. Similar wording does not establish one-to-one release-field identity. The website therefore presents these as candidate source mappings and retains the identifier caveat pending a verified release crosswalk.

## Catalog treatment

- One candidate phenotype concept: `walking_speed`, labeled “计时步行表现（2.5米，两次计时）”.
- Six wave/code fields in the audited catalog: `qg002` and `qg003` for each of 2011, 2013, and 2015; the objective-measurement crosswalk has 12 rows because each code also appears in distinct trial/measurement mappings that must remain visible.
- Role is P-only after the reviewed override; the fields are not exogenous exposures.
- Unit is seconds. Trial identity, eligibility, non-completion codes, source paths, and review state are retained. No measured values, frequencies, associations, or analysis-ready status are claimed.
