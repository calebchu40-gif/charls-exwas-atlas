# 2014 WORKHISTORY F116 and F117 self-employment/military review

## Sources and scope

Reviewed public 2014 Life History Questionnaire and Codebook definitions/routing for F116/F116_a and F117a/F117b families. No participant responses, income distributions, frequency or association analyses were performed.

## F116 self-employment income

The 21 F116/F116_a fields ask monthly net income at the beginning and middle period of non-agricultural own-account self-employment (F_3=4). The questionnaire defines net income as total income minus input costs. Keep beginning and middle phases and job slots. Do not include F_3=5 unpaid household-business help, whose question asks work type rather than F116 income. Values are retrospective socioeconomic measures, not environmental exposure; nominal amounts are not inflation-harmonized.

## F117 military service

For military-service episodes (F_3=6), 12 F117a/F117a_a fields record monthly net income after tax and social-insurance contributions during the first year and middle period. Twelve F117b/F117b_a fields record rank categories at the start and middle periods. Four F117b_a1 fields record the year of change to the middle-period rank, asked only if beginning and middle ranks differ. Episode slots and phase remain distinct; structural skips do not imply zero income or no promotion. These are service/socioeconomic context indicators, not occupational-agent or exposure-dose measures.

## Local catalogue impact

The 49 fields map to four concepts. Local Life History catalogue: 145 concepts / 4,722 field mappings (E=4,676, P=46); WORKHISTORY 1,881 mapped candidate fields and 106 substantive fields deferred. These counts are metadata rows, not analysis-ready indicators or sample sizes; formal association models remain zero.
