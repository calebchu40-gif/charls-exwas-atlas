# Homestead land area

The 2011, 2013, 2015 and 2018 I002 items ask for the area of the homestead associated with the current residence and record square meters. The questionnaire defines homestead as land allocated by a rural collective organization for housing and a small garden; this is not a universal residential lot-area measure.

Questionnaire instructions direct no-entry/not-applicable cases to 0 in the covered forms. Therefore, zero can encode structural nonapplicability rather than a genuinely zero-area lot until the authorized release and eligibility route are checked. Soft checks below 10 or above 1,000 m² are interviewer prompts, not validated analytic limits. Codebook extrema are not used to set cleaning thresholds. The measure remains household/residence context; cross-wave equivalence, special values, routes, linkage and missingness require release-specific QA.

Only official questionnaires and Codebooks were used; no participant data or Codebook extrema were copied. `scripts/apply_homestead_land_area_review.py` propagates the measurement notes through both catalog pipelines.
